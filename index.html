Prototype

Mainstreet Dispatch Prototype

A clickable prototype of two Mainstreet screens, built from the Figma designs and styled with the ONE DS design system. Everything uses sample data. Anything that isn't built yet opens a "not built in this prototype" page with a way back.

local_shipping Transport Dispatch Allocate unallocated pickups and deliveries to drivers by zone.
  • Pick a zone cell, tick jobs, choose a driver, then Allocate
  • Filters panel, saved filters and Refresh
  • Bulk actions: print documents, advise customer
Open Transport Dispatch arrow_forward edit_document Consignment Edit Review and update a consignment: legs, addresses, items and documents.
  • Items grid: add, edit and delete lines; STC, Hire and Shipped As
  • Upload documents in the side sheet; add references
  • Label, Print and Copy split buttons; Save validation
Open Consignment Edit arrow_forward

Getting around

  • Switch screens with the dark Prototype pill at the bottom, or press Alt 1 / 2 (Alt 0 comes back here).
  • Search with ⌘K. Menus in the top bar open on hover.
  • Mainstreet logo in the top-left always brings you back to this page.
